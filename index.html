How it works. Maintenance calories come from the Mifflin-St Jeor BMR equation multiplied by your activity factor, then reduced by the adaptation percentage. The linear line holds your pace fixed forever. The realistic line holds your calories fixed instead, so the deficit narrows as maintenance falls with your weight, which is what actually happens.

Estimates, not medical advice. Real bodies vary by several hundred calories a day, water weight masks fat loss for weeks at a time, and a large deficit belongs under medical supervision. Talk to a doctor before going below the intake floor.
